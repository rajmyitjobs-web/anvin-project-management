Set up mastersOwner: roles, users, customers, types, modules and activities
Create projectManager: customer, stage, people, companies and module scope
Plan company deliveryLead: baseline activities, budget, go-live and AMC obligations
Allocate workManager/lead: daily date + hours or a weekly visit worksheet
Notify and performReview WhatsApp message; employee signs in and performs work
Report actualEmployee: date, hours, outcome, status and client-deduction choice
Review decisionComplete? Review company go-live. More work? Confirm a new allocation
Track and reportManager: timesheets, budget, pending work, AMC and date-range PSR
Confirm go-liveReview all companies/modules; retain history and report results

If work remains: record the reason, obtain client confirmation, then return to allocation. A saved actual update creates or updates timesheet effort; it does not silently schedule another visit.

Who updates what?

Owner configures access. Manager/lead allocates and reviews. Employee reports actual work. Manager approves corrections and client reporting through the configured role rights.
